# Halakhic Principles — Comparative Provenance

Halakhic value hierarchies inform OSD as one comparative tradition among several. **No country is scored under Jewish law.**

## Interpretive parallels

| Halakhic principle | Meaning | OSD Domain |
|--------------------|---------|------------|
| pikuach nefesh | Preservation of life | D2 |
| tzedek | Justice | D6 |
| tzedakah | Welfare / redistribution | D5 |
| kavod habriyot | Human dignity | D7 |
| dina demalkhuta dina | Legitimate civil authority | D8 |
| bal tashchit | Preventing needless destruction | D10 |

## Usage in OSD

These parallels appear in policy mapping (`halakha_parallels` field) to show where Jewish ethical jurisprudence converges with other public-value frameworks. They do not constitute binding legal classification.
